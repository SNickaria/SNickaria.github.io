Dr. Shiva NickAria

Founder & AI Platform Architect at ARIA Therapeutics (ARIA-Tx) | Immuno-Oncology & Genetics

Most therapeutic programmes falter long before they reach patients because clinical presentation, molecular mechanism, and chemical design are rarely examined inside the same framework. I build systems that bring those layers together, evaluating clinical phenotypes and treatment responses alongside genomic, molecular, and chemical architecture to uncover where a disease is genuinely vulnerable and identify the strongest therapeutic strategy for its context.

Over fifteen years across clinical genetics, wet-lab therapeutic development, and computational biology, I have moved from diagnosing rare inherited disorders and developing genome engineering strategies, to designing and building a novel antibody-based immunotherapeutic from scratch during my PhD in immuno-oncology, to building machine-learning pipelines for high-dimensional disease profiling. Today, as Founder and AI Platform Architect at ARIA-Tx, I am translating that multidisciplinary perspective into ARIA-Tx's closed learning loop, architecting generative, predictive, and agentic AI systems for a platform advancing a new class of programmable biotherapeutics across disease and longevity.

Scientific & Technical Capabilities

Generative, Predictive & Agentic AI

Architecting and developing AI/ML systems for ARIA-Tx, integrating biological reasoning, predictive modelling and agentic workflows to support programmable biotherapeutic development across disease and longevity.

Biology- & Chemistry-Aware ML Pipelines

Building end-to-end analytical pipelines to characterise disease biology and chemistry, interpret high-dimensional data, and identify potential therapeutic strategies. Combining molecular profiles, spatial organisation and clinical context to move from patterns towards mechanistic hypotheses.

Precision Medicine Engineering

Designing and building novel therapeutics, connecting mechanistic hypotheses with experimental development and functional validation. Previous work includes a novel antibody-based immunotherapeutic for B-cell malignancies developed during my University of Aberdeen PhD, with a focus on tumour-associated macrophage reprogramming.

Genome Engineering & Genetics

Experience with CRISPR/Cas9 and base-editing approaches in induced pluripotent stem cells (iPSCs) and haematopoietic stem/progenitor cells, grounded in more than a decade of cellular, molecular and clinical genetics and rare disease studies. Research training includes a genome-editing fellowship at the MRC Weatherall Institute of Molecular Medicine, University of Oxford.

Precision Diagnosis

Connecting genomic and molecular findings with clinical presentation to investigate rare inherited disorders and support disease stratification. Experience spans genetic diagnostic workflows, genotype-phenotype analysis, and collaborative interpretation of complex cases.

Selected Research & Development

ARIA-Tx: Programmable Biotherapeutics Platform

Founder-led architecture and development of ARIA-Tx, integrating generative, predictive, and agentic AI systems with therapeutic synthesis, experimental development, and translational therapeutic packages for a new class of programmable biotherapeutics across disease and longevity.

Immunotherapy Development

Developed a novel antibody-based immunotherapeutic during my PhD at the University of Aberdeen, combining therapeutic design, molecular engineering and functional investigation in B-cell malignancies to reprogramme the innate immune system as a therapeutic strategy.

Spatial Profiling & Machine Learning

Developed an end-to-end ML analysis pipeline for high-plex CODEX imaging and spatial profiling of the tumour microenvironment at the University of Manchester, investigating cellular neighbourhoods, cell-cell interactions and immune organisation in bone marrow malignancies.

Precision Diagnostics & Gene and Cell Therapies

Led genetics diagnostics and translational genome engineering programmes at Shiraz University of Medical Sciences, establishing genomic and molecular diagnostic platforms for rare inherited disorders and malignancies alongside multinational clinical research collaborations. Directed genome engineering and CRISPR-based genome editing of stem and progenitor cells as a therapeutic strategy, integrating engineering optimisation, off-target safety assessment, and functional validation.
